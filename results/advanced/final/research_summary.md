# Pundits vs Bookies development summary

## Questions and design

Does preview text improve football outcome probabilities after accounting for bookmaker odds and explicit expert picks? Can text alone recover match outcomes, picks, market probabilities or closing-odds movement? Does assigning text to the home and away teams improve directional information, and do recent football results, author history or nonlinear models improve an odds-based forecast?

The source joins previews and average decimal odds for six leagues by fixture and article ID. The advanced experiments evaluated early and closing markets separately on expanding chronological folds, training from 2122 and validating on 2223, 2324 and 2425. There were 7,364 early and 9,355 closing eligible development fixtures, including 5,484 and 6,979 validation forecasts, respectively. Shared eligibility, strict outcome-release ordering, training-fold preprocessing and fixed H/D/A orientation controlled comparisons. Eligibility is conditional on article/pick coverage and estimated availability. Log loss was primary and Brier secondary. Seasons 2526 and 2627 were reserved from advanced development.

## Development findings

| Phase | Findings |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2, cohort and pilot integrity | The dataset builder enforced strict publication and early-snapshot eligibility. A 100-article pilot found matching sections and picks in later downloads and no conservative post-match text flags. All 100 had post-kickoff modification timestamps. The original HTML remains unavailable, so version availability is unresolved. |
| 3, market calibration | Raw early/closing log loss was 0.980928/0.979448. Market-only residuals gave 0.981282/0.979348 on the initial grid. The early worsening and marginal closing improvement did not indicate a consistent calibration gain. |
| 4, global TF-IDF | Primary and full-article variants used training-only word TF-IDF. Adding primary TF-IDF to market plus picks increased pooled loss by 0.0000098 early and 0.0000009 closing. The full-article ablation also trailed the numerical reference. |
| 5, independent penalties and frozen MiniLM | Separate text penalties did not change the overall result for global TF-IDF. Frozen MiniLM yielded small closing gains over numerical references (0.0000083 without picks and 0.0000106 with picks), with no consistent early or fold-level gain. Pinned-checkpoint historical availability and pretraining overlap were unverified. |
| 06A, implementation verification | Eight residual gradient checks passed with maximum absolute error of 2.0 × 10⁻¹⁰. Phase 05 scores replayed within 7.1 × 10⁻¹⁴. Across five synthetic seeds, increasing L2 shrank noise coefficients and reduced excess loss. At L2 0.03 the planted feature improved loss by 0.12733 alone and 0.09264 among 96 noise features. This verifies recovery at the tested signal strength. |
| 06A, standalone outcomes and pick recovery | Global MiniLM improved class-frequency outcome log loss by 0.00048 early and 0.00065 closing, with unchanged accuracy. Explicit picks scored 1.015966/1.018244 versus class-frequency loss of 1.070227/1.072960. Primary global TF-IDF recovered picks at about 43.8% accuracy versus about 42% for frequencies. Primary MiniLM accuracy was near the frequency reference. Full-article TF-IDF with verdict exposure reached 55.1%/56.5%. |
| 06A, market overlap and movement | Picks explained 64.1%/62.0% of the reduction from mean-market KL. Primary global text recovered 1.6–2.4%. In the pre-snapshot movement cohort, calibration improved on the no-movement reference by 0.58% KL. Market plus picks plus MiniLM gave no consistent improvement on calibration in primary, buffered or retrospective timing groups. Actual quote capture times are unknown. |
| 06B, team-aware text | Separate home/away representations improved directional and standalone tasks. Side TF-IDF achieved 52.33%/52.92% pick accuracy and 31.86%/32.47% market KL recovery, ahead of team MiniLM and both global representations. Standalone outcome loss was 1.061454/1.062173, with explicit picks and market forecasts scoring lower. The verdict parser reproduced its source-derived labels exactly. Adding side TF-IDF to the market-plus-picks residual changed pooled loss by +0.0000017/+0.0000010. Both seven-day intervals crossed zero. |
| 06B, observed team history | As-of Elo, form, venue and rest features improved pooled early loss by 0.000045 versus the early market residual and worsened closing by 0.000020. The paired intervals crossed zero. The matched history missed eight fixtures relative to the observed double-round-robin schedule and omitted other competitions and earlier seasons. |
| 07, author reliability | Shrunk as-of author reliability increased pooled loss for both linear comparisons in both markets. Adding author reliability to market plus picks changed loss by +0.000057/+0.000021. |
| 07, nonlinear modeling | CatBoost on market, picks, observed history, author reliability and side-text SVD lost to raw probabilities in every unblended configuration and fold. All ten market-anchored blends chose alpha 0, reproducing F0. Even alpha 0.1 worsened pooled loss for each block. |

## Freeze and interpretation

F0 anchors the comparison and F1, F2 and F3 successively test calibration, picks and side-aware text. Phase 06B selected side TF-IDF by closing expert-pick recovery, which addresses directional extraction and leaves the incremental outcome question open. Historical features had mixed small changes and incomplete provenance. Author reliability increased loss. Global embeddings showed no reliable incremental benefit, with weaker directional recovery and unresolved provenance. The CatBoost blends added zero weight to the market reference. These findings support freezing the four-model comparison and stopping exploratory searches.

Development folds served both selection and descriptive evaluation. Their paired intervals omit model selection, refit variation and future-season changes. The primary text can include picks. Fixed sentence attribution excludes ambiguous and unassigned sentences, and its private review has no human labels. The whole-corpus legacy alias learner was excluded because it uses future text. The selected representation, tested sample, functional forms and timestamp proxies all constrain inference. The experiments found no reliable incremental improvement beyond bookmaker probabilities. They leave open smaller, time-dependent or differently represented information and do not establish bookmaker market efficiency.
